Telebaia

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026


Telebaia collects no data. Nothing about your child or you leaves the device.

What we collect

Nothing. Telebaia has no accounts and doesn't ask for names, ages, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts or device identifiers.

The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it doesn't connect to the internet, except to the App Store when you buy or restore the full version.

What stays on the device

To work, the app stores a few things on the device only:

  • your settings, such as the language, calm mode, hint text and each app's options;
  • how much of the daily free play each app has used;
  • whether the full version is unlocked (kept by iOS and the App Store).

We can't see this information. It's deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time In-App Purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple only gives us sales reports that don't identify anyone. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Telebaia is made for children aged about 1 to 5. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no ads, chats or links for children to follow. The only links (Terms of Use and this policy) are on the full version purchase screen, which opens only through a slide-to-unlock control meant for parents.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, photos, location or notifications.

Sharing

We have no data, so we share or sell none. The app contains no third-party code that collects data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact me@stichoza.com.
